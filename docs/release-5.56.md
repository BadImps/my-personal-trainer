## My Personal Trainer 5.56

- Adds Workout Library search, Body Area, and Equipment filters to custom exercise selection.
- Repairs the Weekly training-area validation so selected areas move correctly to the calendar stage.
- Home now frames only the current day; earlier days are visibly historical and cannot appear active.
- Guided workouts now offer optional five-minute warm-up and end cool-down choices.
- Warm-up previews the first prescribed movement; cool-down shows Strength Level stretch artwork selected for the workout focus.
- Session completion adds recovery food/drink guidance and a coach recommendation that remains pending in Scheduler until you choose **Apply recommendation**.
