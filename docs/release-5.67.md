# v5.67 — Live workout eligibility

- Checks whether Android has allowed promoted Live Updates instead of assuming the request was accepted.
- Offers the appropriate Android setting the first time a supported workout starts without promotion enabled.
- Adds Settings → Live workout display with a clear enabled, setup-required, or Android-version status.
- Retains the standard ongoing workout notification on devices where Android or the manufacturer does not provide a promoted surface.

Promoted Live Updates require Android 16 or later. Samsung and other manufacturers control the final Now Bar, lock-screen, or status-bar placement and may impose additional eligibility rules.
