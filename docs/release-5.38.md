# v5.38 — Complete workout refinement

- Keeps active guided workouts recoverable through the app and Android notification controls.
- Adds previous, repeat, skip, cooldown, custom-exercise, and untimed-workout behavior.
- Expands training areas and equipment planning with Biceps, Triceps, and Barbell choices.
- Refines workout, cooldown, rest, exercise-report, and upcoming-exercise presentation.
- Expands the Trinidad & Tobago nutrition library and meal-detail routing.
- Unifies the exercise wizard, plan-ready screen, and Exercise Library around neutral Strength
  Level artwork instead of substituting coach portraits.
- Adds and connects additional exact animated exercise loops, including Reverse Lunge, Walking
  Lunge, Cable Hammer Curl, Cable Overhead Extension, Concentration Curl, Diamond Push-up,
  Preacher Curl Machine, Barbell Skull Crusher, and Triceps Kickback.

The current catalog contains genuine multi-frame loops for 72 of 119 unique movements. Static
neutral demonstrations remain available for 33 movements, while 14 specialized movements are
explicitly labelled as awaiting exact artwork instead of showing an incorrect coach or exercise.

This protected OTA is for installations already on v5.32 or later. A legacy v5.31 Migration
installation must complete the documented manual protected-release migration first.
