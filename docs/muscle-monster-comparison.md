# Product comparison: My Personal Trainer and Muscle Monster

Reviewed 2026-10-15 from public product listings only. This is a feature comparison, not a screen, asset, or copy comparison.

| Area | Muscle Monster public positioning | My Personal Trainer improvement | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Personalisation | Goal, fitness level, location and equipment-based plans | Show the exact selected goal, environment and target, then preview a compatible first movement before a plan is created | Reduce surprise and wrong-equipment recommendations |
| Exercise guidance | Large movement/workout catalogue with demonstrations | Offline animated coach GIF at plan selection, library, and guided set; rep callouts plus timed rests | Keep the next action visually clear without a network dependency |
| Progress | Workout history and weight tracking | Local-only profile and private, non-diagnostic weight-trend wording | Privacy-first default and safer health framing |
| Scale | Google Play describes 300+ exercises and 200+ workouts; App Store describes larger figures | Curated 42-movement library with 84 coach-specific animated variants | Prefer a smaller, equipment-aware library until quality and coverage are validated |
| Account and monetisation | In-app purchases are listed publicly | Local account data; no payment flow | Keep onboarding direct and transparent |

## Prioritised roadmap

1. **Build now:** smooth media, an animated selection preview, clearer navigation, tactile controls, cards, and icons (version 1.6).
2. **Next:** explicit fitness-level and session-duration wizard pages, plan editing, substitutions, and saved weekly calendar.
3. **Later:** optional export/backup that is off by default, accessibility settings (reduced motion, larger text, spoken cues), and opt-in wearable/health connections.
