# v5.53 — Completed endurance reports

- Completed walks, runs, and cycles now show a Coach Hero endurance report matching the workout report’s KPI, time-breakdown, and energy-use presentation.
- Adds a private route replay that draws the lime route from the first captured point to the finish while its normalized elapsed time advances from zero to the recorded duration.
- Completed endurance sessions now appear as endurance days on Home—even when they were not scheduled first—and receive the coral **DONE** ribbon.
- Route geometry, replay, and reports remain on-device. Energy figures are broad estimates and require an optional current-weight entry.
