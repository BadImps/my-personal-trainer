# My Personal Trainer v7.10

## Finish a workout without losing the rest of it

- Tap **End workout** for the existing emergency-stop path.
- Press and hold **End workout** to choose **End exercise here**.
- Work completed through the current exercise is recorded in Progress.
- Any later exercises are saved as a same-day **Finish custom workout** on My Calendar, ready to resume when needed.
- Sets, reps, and between-exercise cooldowns use direct sliders in the workout setup.
