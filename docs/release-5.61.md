# v5.61 — Clearer weekly energy and weight progress

- Refines the weekly Energy Estimate into the approved Energy Split layout: Endurance, Guided Workouts, and Combined calories each have their own icon, calorie total, animated rail, and percentage.
- Replaces the calorie-to-weight-equivalent line with a personal weight-progress chart.
- The chart shows logged baseline/current weight, a selected target, the height-based adult BMI reference range when Body details are available, and a trend-only projection after at least two dated check-ins.
- Weight projections use entered scale measurements only; they do not infer scale weight from workouts or calorie estimates.

BMI is an adult screening reference, not a diagnosis or an ideal-weight prescription.
