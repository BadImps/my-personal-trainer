# Caribbean Food Library

The in-app Caribbean Food Library is an offline, searchable reference of common foods and ingredients. It displays rounded energy, protein, carbohydrate, fibre, fat, and sodium values **per 100 g edible portion**.

## Sources and scope

- FAO/INFOODS indexes the *Food Composition Tables for Use in the English-Speaking Caribbean* compiled by the Caribbean Food and Nutrition Institute (CFNI), including the 1995 second edition: <https://www.fao.org/infoods/infoods/tables-and-databases/caribbean/en/>.
- USDA FoodData Central provides structured, documented nutrient reference datasets and exposes the data through its API and downloads: <https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/api-guide/>.

The library is a practical cross-reference based on these composition sources and commonly matched plain foods. It intentionally does not treat a homemade dish, restaurant meal, or branded item as a fixed nutrition fact.

## Important limits

- Values are rounded reference values, not a product-label substitute.
- Cooking method, added oil, coconut milk, sugar, saltfish soaking, seasoning, brand, and portion size can materially change the nutrition of a meal.
- Entries for canned or salted foods identify the preparation and flag sodium-sensitive foods.
- The library does not prescribe calories, portions, weight change, or medical diets. People with allergies, medical conditions, pregnancy, eating concerns, or specific clinical targets should use a clinician or registered dietitian’s advice.
